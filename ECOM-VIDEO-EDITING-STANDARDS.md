# E-commerce Video Editing Standards

## Creative storytelling

- Open on an immediate emotional hook.
- Reveal the product quickly, then demonstrate the product-specific action.
- End on a satisfying functional, visual, or emotional payoff.
- Preserve the reference's important physical actions and storytelling mechanics.

## Editing

- Match the reference's shot timing and editing rhythm where practical.
- Keep source footage duration distinct from its final edited duration; trim to the action, not the generated clip's full length.
- Favor intentional hard cuts and remove pauses that do not add meaning.
- Preserve natural smartphone-camera movement and imperfections.
- Use vertical 9:16 framing. Match the original reference frame rate when practical.

## Typography

- Use the approved Version 2 style in `video-editing/reusable-assets/ECOM — In-Store Reaction — Typography V1/` as the default.
- Preserve its white fill, near-black outline, and restrained shadow treatment.
- Adapt the wording, line breaks, and emojis to each advertisement; reposition the emoji artwork when copy length changes.
- When an uploaded reference has a distinctive typography style, match that reference instead of forcing this default preset.

## Audio

- Preserve synchronized natural effects, including impacts, reactions, and useful ambience.
- Duck music around important physical sound effects so those sounds remain clear.
- Use user-supplied music when provided. Do not substitute arbitrary music when the intended track is unavailable.
- Verify commercial usage rights before publishing.

## Quality control

- Compare the final export against the original reference.
- Inspect physical product interactions and verify character and product continuity.
- Check caption readability and styling at phone size.
- Review audio synchronization and music balance.
- Inspect and play the rendered file; timeline playback alone is not final-export quality control.
