## Ranked causes

1. **Starting-image/video-state mismatch — high confidence for Clip 4.** The image is already bright enough that the requested press-to-bright transformation has no unmistakable baseline.
2. **Starting pose plus action density — high confidence for Clip 1.** Peak surprise is frozen into frame zero, then the prompt repeats a reaction and stacks falling, impact/spill, and a late product pan into five seconds. The rough cut additionally ends before the reveal lands.
3. **Scene-specific creative camera directions — high confidence for Clip 5 and medium for Clip 3.** “Slow handheld push-in,” “shallow depth of field,” and “warm bokeh” pull Clip 5 toward a cinematic look. “As the table lands” adds an unnecessary landing cue to Clip 3.
4. **Video-model geometry limits — medium confidence for Clips 2 and 5.** Packaging/product detail and miniature layouts can drift despite correct references. Avoid adding more negative prompt text; shorten exposure to moving product detail or simplify the motion.
5. **Audio — low confidence as a failure source for Clip 1.** The output contains audio and the impact transient is near the visible cup contact. Keep event/audio sync explicit; no soundtrack or effects are needed.

The current saved plan asks for “recreate ... exactly” while also replacing the reference cast and adding a coffee drop. The planner should preserve compatible story beats and camera rhythm while following the target scene's actual frame-zero state, actions, cast, and product; incompatible reference actions should not override these.

## Production rules now in code

`src/ecom_video/creative_playbook.py` now gives In-Store Reaction planning concise rules for proportionate cause-directed reactions, one primary physical beat at natural speed, credible grip/support/weight/gravity/contact, reference-matched handheld movement, stable product/material/light appearance, and selected native sound synchronized to visible events. `src/ecom_video/workflow.py` applies the short video-production rules only to In-Store Reaction prompts, omits exact duplicate action/product-interaction text, and keeps non-In-Store video prompt behavior on its previous path. `src/ecom_video/providers/openai.py` now scopes the In-Store planning instructions and lessons to that hook instead of injecting store/drop/reveal directions into other formats.

The guidance is generic: no coffee, remote, Christmas-village, fixed camera direction, or fixed clip count is made a universal rule. Other hook formats are covered by regression tests for absence of the new In-Store-only instructions.
